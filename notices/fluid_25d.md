# fluid_25d

The picture — a torch whose flame and smoke read as a lit volume, and a pale fog that flows around a pillar — follows a visual idea by [@r4nM4_64](https://x.com/r4nM4_64/status/2120431026191344067), including the Zelda: Ocarina of Time fog study that post quotes. No code was taken from those posts. The author describes the picture as a 2D fluid plus a ray march, and points at the talk below for the pressure solve. His demo does not use a depth buffer for occlusion, and neither does this sample.

The motion is Stam's stable fluids (Jos Stam, "Stable Fluids", SIGGRAPH 1999): an impulse, semi-Lagrangian advection, buoyancy, vorticity confinement, a pressure solve, then a gradient subtracted from the velocity. Density is sharpened with one MacCormack step. The pressure solve is not a Jacobi loop. It is two separable convolutions, the rank-1 reduction of the Jacobi impulse response described by Rabbani and Khiat, "Fast Eulerian Fluid Simulation In Games Using Poisson Filters", SCA 2020, [10.2312/sca.20201221](https://doi.org/10.2312/sca.20201221). That paper is © 2020 The Eurographics Association (Creative Commons covers Eurographics releases from 2022 on), and it publishes no kernel table. The 11 weights in the sample are the truncated first singular vector of 32 Jacobi iterations of the same 5-point update, derived here.

The picture ray-marches a slab. Density is a thickness about the plane of the grid, symmetric in depth, and a short march accumulates emission, absorption and a few samples toward the light. That reading is ours, from the author's description; no shader was published. The fog's thickness is a noise texture this sample bakes once, three octaves in three passes, and samples while shading. It is not part of the paper. Each octave's period divides the grid, the hash wraps on that period, and the shear is applied to the sample coordinate by a whole number of cells, so the texture tiles. The shade reads it through a wrapped filter. The torch is a narrow flame with grey smoke where the fire cools, over a dim warm haze. The fog is a soft grey-blue veil, lit a little from above, with a small soft opening on the pillar.
