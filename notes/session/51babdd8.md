## 🤖 Assistant

For **immutable globals** with constant initializers, modern WASM JIT compilers (wasmtime, V8, SpiderMonkey) will constant-fold them at compile time, making `global.get $kind_int` effectively equivalent to `i64.const 1` in performance.

However:

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| `i64.const 1` | Smaller bytecode, fastest interpretation | Magic numbers in output |
| `global.get $kind_int` | Self-documenting, readable `wasm2wat` | Slightly larger file, slower in interpreters |

The globals are valuable for:
- **Debugging**: `wasm-tools print` shows meaningful names
- **Validation**: Can verify the constants match your Rust enums
- **Tooling**: Debuggers/profilers can reference them

Practically, keep the globals for documentation. For hot paths where every nanosecond matters, you could have a `--release` mode that inlines them, but it's likely premature optimization - the JIT handles it.
