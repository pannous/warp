Next isolate which declaration in lib/time.warp interacts with units and find a general library fix, then reapply and test conversion. The library declares the `ms()` unit. This points to a field/unit resolution interaction, though its core location is not yet established. The temporary module was restored exactly. Next investigate general field shadowing and resolution (including a differently named field/unit pair) before judging whether a library solution preserves the public `.ms` field.
