    Unicode → Uniscript

      Syntax: <:name> or \:name for one entity (Unicode, LaTeX or HTML names), <:block operands> or <:block> … <:/block> for a style, stacked effects like <:mirror red A>. Colors and geometry are invisible TAG characters that the Uniscript fonts (Noto-derived, OFL) render; NewGardinerOmni (OFL) joins hieroglyphs. Spec: wiki/uniscript.md.
